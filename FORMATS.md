# Escala Brutal: formats and variety rules

The channel is about **things that blow your mind by their scale**, not
only animal sizes. Mix formats every day.

## Variety rules (owner's words: "no seas monotema, ve variando día a día,
## pero si algo funciona de locos, sigue con ello; lo único, no te emperres")
1. **Follow the data, not a quota.** If a format or theme clearly
   outperforms the rest (views, retention), give it more slots (it can even
   be 2 of the 3 videos), but keep varying the angle, subject and hook.
2. **No monotony.** Never the same format three days in a row, and never 3
   videos on the same theme the same day unless the data justifies it.
3. **Don't get stuck.** If a format underperforms for a week, retire it or
   change it; if a winner starts dropping, rotate it out for a while.
4. **Keep exploring.** Video **c** is the experimental slot most days: a new
   format from "To test", or an unusual angle nobody does in Spanish. Log how
   it did. (Skip the experiment only when doubling down on a clear winner.)
5. **Something current** most days: a trend, news-safe event, anniversary,
   release, season, sport or space mission.
6. Vary hooks and outro lines; don't repeat the same one twice in a row.
7. Originality check before writing: if the idea is a copy of a typical
   video in the niche, find a twist (an unexpected comparison, a personal
   "you" angle, a surprising number, a reveal at the end).
8. Every Monday: promote winners, retire losers, add at least one new format
   to "To test".

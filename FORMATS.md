# Escala Brutal: formats and variety rules

The channel is about **things that blow your mind by their scale**, not
only animal sizes. Mix formats every day.

## Variety rules (mandatory)
1. Each day: 3 videos in **3 different formats**.
2. **Max 1 animal video per day**, and not the same category two days in a row.
3. Never repeat a hook style, outro line or main colour of footage twice in a row.
4. Video **c** is the **experimental slot**: a new format from the "To test"
   list, or a new angle nobody does in Spanish. Log how it did.
5. At least **1 video per day tied to something current** (trend, news-safe
   event, anniversary, release, season, sport, space mission).
6. Every week, add at least one new format to "To test" and retire one that
   underperforms.
7. Originality check before writing: if the idea is a copy of a typical
   video in the niche, find a twist (an unexpected comparison, a personal
   "you" angle, a surprising number, a reveal at the end).

## Active formats
| Format | Idea | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Escala | Size/weight/distance next to things you know (3D scene) | Cachalote vs autobús |
| Dinero a escala | Fortunes, prices, salaries made tangible | 1 billón en billetes de 100 € apilados |
| Tiempo a escala | Durations that break your head | Si la historia de la Tierra fuera 1 día |
| ¿Qué pasaría si...? | Science-based hypotheticals (conservative) | Si la Luna estuviera a la distancia de la ISS |
| Tu cuerpo a escala | Human body numbers | Tus vasos sanguíneos darían 2 vueltas a la Tierra |
| Lugares imposibles | Real places with extreme numbers | El pueblo más frío habitado (Oymyakon) |
| Máquinas brutales | Biggest/fastest human-made things | La excavadora Bagger 288 vs un edificio |
| Récords del planeta | Extremes of nature | El rayo más largo registrado |
| Historia a escala | Historical facts with surprising scale | Cleopatra está más cerca del iPhone que de las pirámides |

## To test (experimental slot)
- **Ranking cuenta atrás** (5 → 1, final reveal)
- **Mito o realidad** (claim on screen, reveal with the number)
- **Antes vs ahora** (how something changed in scale over time)
- **Lo que pasa en 1 segundo en el mundo**
- **Adivina cuál es más grande** (two options, pause, reveal)
- **Precio real de...** (what something really costs to make or run)
